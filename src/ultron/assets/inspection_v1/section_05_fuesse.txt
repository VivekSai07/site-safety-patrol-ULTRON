Section 5: Leiter-/Trittfüße/Rollen / feet and rollers

fuesse.vollstaendigkeit — Vollständigkeit/Befestigung [mode=visual]
Inspect every required foot/roller seen, including rear feet; obscured feet remain unknown.

fuesse.abnutzung — Abnutzung/Beschädigung [mode=visual]
Inspect visible foot-cap tears, tread wear and roller damage; keep ground-contact context.

fuesse.funktion — Funktionsfähigkeit [mode=person]
Grip, brake function and loaded stability require physical checks; apparent floor contact is insufficient.

Only verified applicability may yield N_A. Unseen components are UNKNOWN, not intact.
