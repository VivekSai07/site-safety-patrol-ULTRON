Section 4: Beschlagteile / fittings

beschlag.beschaedigung — Beschädigung/Korrosion [mode=visual]
Describe visible rust, cracks or deformation on hinges/brackets without guessing hidden corrosion.

beschlag.vollstaendigkeit — Vollständigkeit/Befestigung [mode=visual]
Identify visibly absent fittings/fasteners; no torque or tightness claim.

beschlag.funktion — Funktionsfähigkeit [mode=person]
Do not claim moving mechanisms function from one or multiple static images.

beschlag.abnutzung — Abnutzung [mode=visual]
Inspect visible worn guides/hinges/contact faces; play is not inferred without a test.

beschlag.schmierung — Schmierung (mechanische Teile) [mode=person]
Neither shine nor a visible smear proves adequate lubrication or mechanism function.

Only verified applicability may yield N_A. Unseen components are UNKNOWN, not intact.
