Section 1: Holme / rails

holme.verformung — Verformung [mode=visual]
Look for bowed, kinked or twisted rails; compare continuous rail edges.

holme.beschaedigung — Beschädigung (z.B. Risse) [mode=visual]
Inspect visible rail surfaces for cracks, splits and impact damage; dirt alone is not a crack.

holme.kanten — Scharfe Kanten, Splitter, Grat [mode=person]
Visible burrs or splinters may be reported as damage; absence of sharpness requires a person.

holme.abnutzung — Abnutzung [mode=visual]
Distinguish worn material from cosmetic scuffs and lighting; hidden thickness is unknown.

holme.schutz — Schutzbehandlung (bei Holz) [mode=person]
Wood treatment adequacy cannot be established from colour/shine. Visible peeling is an observation only.
