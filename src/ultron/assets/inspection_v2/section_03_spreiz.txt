Section 3: Spreizsicherung / spreader

spreiz.vollstaendigkeit — Vollständigkeit/Befestigung [mode=visual]
Inspect both spreader endpoints and presence. Visible attachment does not prove security.

spreiz.funktion — Funktionsfähigkeit [mode=person]
A still image cannot verify operation, locking or load resistance. Default NOT_CHECKED unless literal visible damage supports DEFECT.

spreiz.beschaedigung — Beschädigung [mode=visual]
Inspect spreader links/straps for visible breaks, bends or tears.

Only verified applicability may yield N_A. Unseen components are UNKNOWN, not intact.
